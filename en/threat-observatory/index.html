DATA TRUTH CONTRACT // NO CINEMA FAKES

Looks cinematic.
Still grounded in verifiable data.

The map may be spectacularly animated, but every technical point has a real source and a clear scope. It is expressly not a complete scan of the Internet. Private servers are neither searched nor displayed. Publicly visible infrastructure points come from the voluntary RIPE Atlas measurement network; their positions are already spatially obscured by RIPE to protect the operators.

SOURCE // 01

Natural Earth

The high-resolution country geometry is based on the freely usable 1:50m data set. It serves only as a cartographic basis and does not claim network activity.

RIPE Atlas
