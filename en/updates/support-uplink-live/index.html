01 // SUPPORT-01

Voluntary support with clear steps

One-off support is possible from five euros. JARVIS VIP costs six euros per month, FOUNDING SUPPORTER costs sixteen euros per month. The community and public access to the project remain free.

02 // SUPPORT-01

Official channels instead of scattered payment links

The support portal documents the model, limits and rules. Payments are made exclusively via the official Ko-fi profile JARVIS SYSTEM. Website, GitHub, Discord, X and LinkedIn point to the same traceable origin.

03 // SUPPORT-01

Discord roles tied to memberships

The Ko-fi Discord integration is configured: JARVIS VIP and FOUNDING SUPPORTER have their intended roles. The practical delivery after a real payment is checked and documented in the first real support case.

04 // SUPPORT-01

Founding 100 without false promises

Permanent recognition only arises after three successful paid months and an express opt-in for the name or alias. Founding Supporter does not mean company founder and does not create shares, team, tester, admin, ownership or governance rights.

05 // SUPPORT-01

Transparency remains more important than show values

Cost coverage, number of supporters and revenue remain N/A until proven data is available. JARVIS SYSTEM does not publish any fictitious financial values and does not describe voluntary support as an investment or as a tax-deductible donation.
