07 // MANAGED HOSTING

Hosting only when the operation is reliably secured

Own servers alone do not ensure reliable customer hosting. That's why every possible website recording is technically checked and only approved after clear operational and security acceptance.

Suitable websites include HTTPS, separate customer areas, monitoring, backups, restore, updates and a clean migration path. Static websites are checked first; complex services remain individual decisions.

  • Static websites and controlled web presences first
  • Domains should, if possible, be owned by the customer
  • Backups with verifiable recovery path
  • No open root access or uncontrolled databases
  • No anonymous proxies, mining, GPU rental or unlawful services
CURRENT STATUS // Managed hosting is only available after technical acceptance and a written service definition.

08 // SPECIAL FUNCTIONS

Shop, login or upload? We deliberately plan this separately

Shops, customer accounts, bookings, file uploads and sensitive data require more protection, operation and legal clarification than a normal information website.

Such functions are openly planned as an additional expansion stage. If safe operation is not yet responsible, we scale down the first step or honestly reject the function.

09 // SHORT ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions before your first inquiry

The most important points in everyday language. Technical and legal details only become relevant when they actually affect your specific project.

Do I already know what technology I need?

No. Describe your goal or problem. We only propose solutions that fit the confirmed scope and realistic operations.

Does the first inquiry cost anything?

No. The initial written inquiry is non-binding. Costs only arise after a concrete offer or if a fixed price service has been expressly booked.

Does JARVIS SYSTEM only work for craft businesses?

No. Companies, self-employed people, clubs, founders, organizations and private individuals from every suitable industry can inquire.

Can my existing website continue to be used?

Often yes. First, it is checked whether a targeted redesign makes more sense than a completely new building.

Is managed hosting already available for every project?

No. Hosting is only released in writing after technical recording, backup, security and operational checks.

Do I need access data for the first contact?

No. Please do not send passwords, recovery codes or confidential data. Accesses will only be clarified later in a controlled manner for a confirmed order.
