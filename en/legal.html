The public information website does not use its own analytics, advertising or profiling services, user accounts or transmitting contact form. It does not store tracking cookies. As with any web request, hosting and network providers may process necessary connection metadata.

Technical recipients may include GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Fourthwall and their payment, production, shipping, security and support providers. Processing may take place in the United States and, depending on the delivery and production route, in other countries. The privacy roles and policies identified there also apply to the checkout operated by Fourthwall. Retention and deletion depend on the relevant purpose, statutory obligations and the policies of the integrated platforms. Details for the hosted shop: Privacy, Terms and Returns & FAQ.

07 // SHOP & ORDERS

Shop, payment and production

The provider of the JARVIS product presentation and the responsible brand and project contact is Oliver-frank Pristaff – JARVIS SYSTEM, Gallusstrasse 1, 9403 Goldach, Switzerland. Purchases in the branded checkout are processed by Fourthwall as Merchant of Record. Fourthwall processes the order there and coordinates payment, applicable sales tax or VAT, production, shipping status and technical order support. The contract, pricing and provider information shown in checkout immediately before the order remains authoritative for the specific transaction. JARVIS does not present these tasks as its own textile production.

Product, size and quantity can be changed or removed in the JARVIS cart before proceeding. Clicking “Checkout” does not yet place an order or trigger payment. Entries can be reviewed and corrected again in the hosted checkout. Only the final action clearly marked as requiring payment submits the binding order; an electronic confirmation is then sent.

The JARVIS catalogue and branded checkout use CHF as the transparent offer currency. The product price, specific shipping costs, applicable taxes and total amount are shown before the order with an obligation to pay. A card issuer may apply its own conversion or fees when the account uses another currency.

International sales are limited to the countries and products actually selectable in the current Fourthwall checkout. Supported destinations may change. Cross-border deliveries may incur import duties, taxes or carrier charges unless checkout explicitly shows them as already collected. Such amounts may be requested by customs authorities or the carrier.

Apparel is produced after an order is placed. The platform provider states a typical production time of three to five business days before dispatch; international transit may take two to four weeks after production. These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. Tracking is provided by email after dispatch.

Please check the size chart before purchasing. Where the consumer's country of residence or delivery does not provide a mandatory withdrawal right, no additional voluntary return is offered for made-to-order items solely because of a change of mind or an incorrectly selected size. EU consumers generally have the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal from receipt of the goods; an exception is claimed only when an item was genuinely personalised to the customer's specifications and applicable law permits it. The standard CORE SERIES 01 products are not treated as personalised merely because they are produced on demand.

For the platform process, printing, quality, incorrect-delivery or transit damage should be reported within 30 days of delivery with the order number and clear photographs. This process period and any right of withdrawal are separate; mandatory statutory defect, warranty, refund and consumer rights in the relevant country remain unaffected.
